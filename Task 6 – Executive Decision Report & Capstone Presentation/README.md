
# Task 6 – Executive Decision Report & Capstone Presentation

## Project Overview

This project synthesizes the findings from the retail business analytics portfolio into an executive-ready Business Intelligence report.

The objective is to convert analytical insights into actionable strategic business decisions for improving revenue, profitability, customer experience, and operational performance.

## Business Problem

The business needs a clear understanding of:

- Overall sales and order performance
- Revenue drivers
- Product category performance
- Regional sales performance
- Customer gender contribution
- Payment method usage
- Return order patterns
- Monthly sales trends
- Profitability and business risks

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub

## Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Calculated Sales
- Estimated Profit
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Gender
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Returned vs Non-Returned Orders
- Sales by Payment Method

## Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard contains:

- Interactive KPI cards
- Category-wise sales analysis
- Region-wise sales analysis
- Customer gender analysis
- Monthly sales trend
- Returned and non-returned order analysis
- Payment method analysis
- Price vs calculated sales scatter chart
- Category and region slicers

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Improve High-Performing Product Categories

Focus marketing, inventory, and promotional activities on categories generating higher sales.

### 2. Reduce Product Returns

Analyze return reasons, delivery performance, and customer complaints to reduce return-related revenue loss.

### 3. Optimize Payment and Regional Strategies

Strengthen commonly used payment methods and improve sales strategies in high-performing regions.

## Implementation Timeline

### Phase 3 – Monitoring and Evaluation
