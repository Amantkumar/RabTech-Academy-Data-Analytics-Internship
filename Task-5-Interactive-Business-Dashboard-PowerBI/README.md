
# Task 5 – Interactive Business Dashboard & KPI Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **RabTech Academy Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task was to create an interactive business dashboard using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides a clear overview of business performance through KPIs, charts, sales analysis, customer analysis, payment method analysis, and interactive filters.

The dashboard contains two pages:

* **Page 1 – Business Overview**
* **Page 2 – Detailed Sales and Customer Analysis**

## Tools and Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence
* KPI Analysis
* Data Analysis

## Dataset

The dashboard was created using a cleaned retail business dataset.

The dataset contains information related to:

* Order ID
* Product ID
* Category
* Price
* Quantity
* Total Amount
* Calculated Sales
* Estimated Profit
* Customer Age
* Customer Gender
* Region
* Payment Method
* Order Date
* Returned Orders

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard contains the following important KPIs:

1. **Total Sales**
2. **Total Orders**
3. **Calculated Sales**
4. **Estimated Profit**

These KPI cards provide a quick summary of the overall business performance.

## Page 1 – Business Overview

The first page provides a high-level overview of sales and business performance.

### Visualizations Included

* Total Sales KPI Card
* Total Orders KPI Card
* Calculated Sales KPI Card
* Estimated Profit KPI Card
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Sales by Customer Gender
* Monthly Sales Trend

### Page 1 Analysis

The visuals help analyze:

* Sales performance across product categories
* Regional sales contribution
* Customer gender-wise sales distribution
* Monthly sales trends
* Overall orders and profit performance

## Page 2 – Detailed Sales and Customer Analysis

The second page provides detailed information about customer behavior, payment methods, regional performance, and sales relationships.

### Visualizations Included

* Returned vs Non-Returned Orders
* Sales by Payment Method
* Sales by Region and Gender
* Price vs Calculated Sales Scatter Chart
* Category Slicer
* Region Slicer

### Scatter Chart

The **Price vs Calculated Sales** scatter chart is used to understand the relationship between product price and calculated sales.

The scatter chart uses:

* Price on the X-axis
* Calculated Sales on the Y-axis
* Quantity as bubble size
* Category as the legend

This visual helps identify sales patterns across different product categories.

### Interactive Slicers

Two interactive slicers are included in the dashboard:

#### 1. Filter by Category

This slicer allows users to filter the dashboard according to product categories such as:

* Beauty
* Electronics
* Fashion
* Grocery
* Home
* Sports
* Toys

#### 2. Filter by Region

This slicer allows users to filter the dashboard according to regions such as:

* Central
* East
* North
* South
* West

The slicers make the dashboard interactive and allow users to explore specific business segments.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

* Overall sales and order performance
* Sales contribution from different product categories
* Regional sales performance
* Monthly sales fluctuations
* Customer gender-wise sales distribution
* Most used payment methods
* Returned and non-returned order patterns
* Relationship between price and calculated sales
* Category-wise and region-wise business performance
